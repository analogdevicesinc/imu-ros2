\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Computes covariance online using Welford\textquotesingle{}s algorithm. 

This provider collects samples during the calibration phrase (IMU should be stationary) and computes variance using Welford\textquotesingle{}s numerically stable online algorithm. After calibration\+\_\+samples are collected, the covariance is frozen and returned for all subsequent queries.

Reference\+: Welford, B. P. (1962). "{}\+Note on method for calculating corrected sum of squares and products"{}
